## About

This engaging quiz application features 20 carefully curated questions about Cameroon's football achievements, legendary players, and historical milestones. Test your knowledge of the Indomitable Lions - from their historic World Cup appearances to AFCON victories and football icons like Roger Milla, Samuel Eto'o, and Thomas N'kono.

Cameroon football has a rich, inspiring history that deserves celebration. From being the first African nation to reach the FIFA World Cup quarter-finals in 1990 to producing legends who changed the game globally, the Indomitable Lions have left an indelible mark on football history.

## Features

- 20 challenging questions covering World Cup history, AFCON titles, legendary players, and club achievements
- Real-time scoring with immediate visual feedback (green for correct, red for incorrect)
- Dynamic progress bar showing your journey through the quiz
- Personalized performance messages based on your final score
- Fully responsive design - works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- Smooth animations and elegant transitions between quiz panels
- No external dependencies - pure vanilla JavaScript
