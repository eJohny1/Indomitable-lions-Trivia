## 📋 Overview

This engaging quiz application features **20 carefully curated questions** about Cameroon's football achievements, legendary players, and historical milestones. Test your knowledge of the Indomitable Lions - from their historic World Cup appearances to AFCON victories and football icons like Roger Milla, Samuel Eto'o, and Thomas N'kono.

## ✨ Features

- **20 Challenging Questions** - Covering World Cup history, AFCON titles, legendary players, and club achievements
- **Real-time Scoring** - Earn points for each correct answer
- **Visual Feedback** - Green/red highlights for correct/incorrect answers
- **Progress Tracking** - Dynamic progress bar shows your journey through the quiz
- **Performance Messages** - Personalized feedback based on your final score
- **Responsive Design** - Fully playable on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- **Smooth Animations** - Elegant transitions between quiz panels

## 🎯 Question Categories

The quiz covers various aspects of Cameroon football:

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| 📅 **Historical Milestones** | First World Cup qualification, Olympic gold medal year |
| 🥅 **Legendary Goalkeepers** | Thomas N'kono, Joseph-Antoine Bell |
| 🏆 **AFCON Achievements** | Title victories, winning years, captaincy |
| ⚽ **World Cup History** | Best performances, famous goals, oldest goalscorers |
| 🌟 **Football Icons** | Samuel Eto'o, Roger Milla, Patrick Mboma |
| 🏟️ **Club & Stadium Knowledge** | African Champions League winners, home stadium |
| 📊 **Statistics** | All-time top scorer, World Cup appearances |
